FAQ

Urbana questions.

Don't see yours? Get in touch or call us.

Can you soft-wash older painted wood siding without stripping the paint?

Yes. Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution to lift algae and mildew — not the force of the water. Older painted wood that would be damaged by high-pressure washing handles soft wash cleanly without any paint or caulk loss.

Do you clean brick homes in Urbana?

Yes. Brick gets a different approach than vinyl — gentler pressure, longer dwell time for the cleaning solution, and rinse direction that avoids forcing water into mortar joints. Many of Urbana's older brick homes near downtown and the campus area are exactly the type we handle.

What about limestone sidewalk slabs — will pressure washing damage them?

Not when done at the right pressure. Limestone is softer than poured concrete, so we lower the pressure and widen the tip. The algae and mildew come off cleanly without etching the surface.
