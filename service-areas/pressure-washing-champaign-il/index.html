FAQ

Champaign questions.

Don't see yours? Get in touch.

Do you serve all of Champaign, including the campus-area neighborhoods?

Yes — we cover all three Champaign zip codes: 61820, 61821, and 61822. That includes the campus corridors, the older West Side residential streets, and the newer subdivisions on the city's edges.

How quickly does algae come back on Champaign siding after a wash?

In central Illinois, algae and mildew typically return in one to two growing seasons on north-facing siding with heavy tree cover. Annual washing is the norm for most Champaign homes surrounded by mature trees.

Can you clean an older concrete driveway without etching it?

Yes. We use a flat surface cleaner, not a direct wand, so pressure is distributed evenly across the slab. This produces better results and avoids the etching risk from aggressive wand use on older concrete.
