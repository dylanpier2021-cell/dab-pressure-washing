Why Urbana homeowners pick us

Older homes need a different approach.

The most common call we get from Urbana homeowners is about north-facing siding or masonry that looks gray-green by midsummer. On older painted wood, biological growth sets in faster than on newer vinyl — the paint is more porous and tree cover keeps surfaces damp longer in spring and fall. Soft washing uses cleaning solution and low pressure to lift the growth without touching the paint. For brick homes, we use gentler pressure and longer dwell time, rinsing in a direction that keeps water out of mortar joints.
