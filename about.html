About DAB

Pressure washing, done right —
by people who actually live here.

We're not a franchise. We're not a side hustle. We don't dabble in five other trades. DAB Pressure Washing is one trade — pressure washing — for homeowners and businesses across Champaign County.

How we started

One trade. Done right.

DAB Pressure Washing was started by two ambitious young entrepreneurs who grew up in Champaign County and got tired of seeing dingy driveways, algae-streaked siding, and storefronts that didn't match how good the businesses inside actually were. They picked up a pressure washer, learned the right pressure for every surface, and started making the town look the way it should.

Today Artem and Barak run DAB full-time, serving homeowners and businesses across all of Champaign County — same focus, same standards, same goal: make Champaign County the cleanest county in central Illinois.

What we believe

Four things we don't compromise on.

Use the right pressure

A house isn't a driveway. Vinyl siding isn't brick. We adjust pressure, tip, and cleaning chemistry for what we are actually cleaning — soft wash for siding, surface cleaner for concrete, controlled pressure for wood. That is why your house won't look streaked when we leave.

Tell you the price first

No "we'll figure it out when we get there." We walk the property, we tell you what it costs, we put it in writing. You decide. No obligation to book.

Show up on time

If we say Tuesday at 9, we're there Tuesday at 9. If something changes, we call before we're late. That shouldn't need to be said, but apparently it does in this trade.

Stay in our lane

If a job needs roof replacement, deck staining, or anything outside what we do well, we'll tell you. We would rather refer you out than do something half-right.

Where we work

We live and work in Champaign County.

If you're in the county, we can get to you. If you're a little outside it, ask anyway — we travel for the right job.
