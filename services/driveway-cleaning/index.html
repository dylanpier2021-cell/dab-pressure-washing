What's included

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Standard driveway cleaning covers the full slab plus edges and any adjacent sidewalk sections. Add-ons like rust pre-treatment or algae post-treatment are priced separately and called out in the quote.

Pre-treatment

Oil, rust, and organic stains get a targeted pre-treatment before the surface cleaner runs. Old, deeply soaked stains may lighten rather than fully disappear — we say so up front.

Surface-cleaner pass

A flat surface cleaner distributes pressure evenly across the slab. No wand zebra lines. Consistent results edge to edge.

Edge and crack detail

Expansion joints, edges, and any spots the surface cleaner can't reach get hand-detailed with a wand.

Final rinse

Slab rinsed clean. Adjacent grass and beds rinsed of any debris pushed off the concrete.

Optional post-treatment

For driveways with chronic algae near downspouts or shaded sections, a post-treatment application slows regrowth. Add it in the quote.

Walk-around

Before we leave, we walk the slab with you. Anything that didn't come up right gets another pass.

FAQ

Driveway cleaning questions.

Will it remove every oil stain?

Most. Fresh stains usually come fully out. Old, deeply soaked stains may lighten significantly but not vanish — we tell you up front during the quote so you are not surprised.

Will pressure washing damage the concrete?

Not when done with a surface cleaner at the right pressure. Aggressive wand-only cleaning by an inexperienced operator can etch concrete — that is not how we work.

How long does it take to dry?

A few hours in summer, longer if it is overcast. You can usually walk on it within an hour and park on it within a couple of hours.

What's the difference between driveway cleaning and concrete cleaning?

Driveways are the most common concrete cleaning job, but the same technique applies to patios, sidewalks, pool decks, and garage floors. If you need sidewalks or a patio done at the same time, see our concrete cleaning service.
