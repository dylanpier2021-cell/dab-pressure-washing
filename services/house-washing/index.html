What's included

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

A standard house wash covers the full exterior cleanable from the ground. Multi-story homes with second-story siding are no problem.

Pre-treatment

Algae, mildew, and mold get a surfactant pre-treatment that does the bulk of the cleaning before any rinse.

Soft-wash rinse

Low-pressure rinse on siding, soffits, fascia, and trim — set for the surface, never just maxed out.

Window exterior rinse

Outside of the windows gets rinsed as part of the wash. Spotting and streaking on the glass after the wash is part of the job.

Plant protection

Plants and beds get pre-soaked before solution goes up and rinsed again after. We talk through anything that needs covered.

Gutter face cleaning

Black tiger stripes on the front of the gutters get treated. Inside-the-gutter cleaning is a separate add-on if you need it.

Walk-around

Before we leave, we walk the house with you. Anything that needs another pass gets it before we pack up.

Signs it's time

You probably need a house wash if…

  • 1

    Green or black streaks running down the north or shaded side of the house.

  • 2

    Black "tiger stripes" running down the face of the gutters.

  • 3

    Cobwebs and dirt building up around eaves, soffits, and porch ceilings.

  • 4

    You are getting the house ready to sell or repaint and need a clean surface to start from.

FAQ

House Washing questions.

Will soft washing damage my siding or paint?

No — soft washing is specifically designed for residential siding. The cleaning solution does the work at low pressure, so there is no risk of forcing water behind siding or stripping paint.

How long does it take?

Most single-story homes run two to three hours. Two-story homes with a lot of siding take longer. We give you a realistic time window in the quote.

How often should I have it done?

Once a year is normal in central Illinois. Humidity, mature trees, and shade let algae come back fairly quickly on north-facing walls.
