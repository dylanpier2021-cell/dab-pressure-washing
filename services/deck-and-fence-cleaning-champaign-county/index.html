What's included

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Deck and fence cleaning includes inspection, cleaning, and a stain-readiness recommendation. Stain and seal work is separate unless quoted together.

Pre-job inspection

Before we touch the wood, we check for rot, loose boards, and protruding nails. Anything we spot gets flagged before we apply pressure.

Wood-safe cleaning solution

Cleaner applied and allowed to dwell. Wood doesn't respond well to brute force — the chemistry step matters as much as the rinse.

Right pressure, right tip

Cedar, pine, and pressure-treated lumber each get a different tip. Composite gets a different approach. Pressure stays in a range that cleans without etching.

Cleanup around the deck

Beds and grass around the deck or fence get rinsed of any cleaner runoff and debris.

Stain-readiness call

When we finish, we tell you whether the wood is ready for stain in 24, 48, or 72 hours based on weather and what we just did to it.

Walk-around

Final walk before we pack up. Anything that needs another pass gets it.

Signs it's time

You probably need deck or fence cleaning if…

  • 1

    Wood looks gray or weathered instead of its original color.

  • 2

    Black or green spotting on deck boards, rails, or fence pickets — algae and mildew, not just dirt.

  • 3

    The deck feels slick underfoot — that is algae buildup, not just dust.

  • 4

    Planning to re-stain or seal — the surface needs to be cleaned first or the new stain won't adhere properly.

FAQ

Deck & fence cleaning questions.

Can I stain or seal right after the deck is cleaned?

Wood typically needs 24 to 72 hours of dry time before staining or sealing, depending on weather. We tell you what to expect for your specific deck when we finish.

Will pressure washing damage softer wood like cedar or pine?

It can if the wrong tip and pressure are used. We adjust both for the wood — cedar and pine get treated more carefully than pressure-treated lumber. Composite gets a different approach again.

How often should a deck be cleaned in Champaign County?

Once a year is typical for most wood decks in central Illinois. Composite decks in shaded areas may need it every one to two years. Our post on how often to pressure wash covers frequency in more detail.
