What's included

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Standard window cleaning covers all accessible exterior windows from the ground. Multi-story access and interior cleaning available — mention it in your quote request.

Pre-job inspection

We check window condition, frame integrity, and caulk seal before we start. Anything we spot that needs attention gets flagged before we apply any solution.

Screen removal & cleaning

Screens are removed, rinsed, and set aside. They get reinstalled when the glass is dry. Dirty screens left on during window washing transfer grime back onto the glass.

Low-pressure exterior wash

Biodegradable cleaning solution, low-pressure rinse. No direct high-pressure spray at seals, frames, or weatherstripping.

Frame and sill cleaning

Frames and sills get wiped down along with the glass. Leaving dirty frames on clean glass defeats the purpose.

Streak-check rinse

Final rinse and visual check from multiple angles before we move to the next window. Streaks get addressed before we pack up.

Walk-around

We walk you through the finished windows before leaving. Anything that needs another pass gets it on the spot.

Signs it's time

You probably need a window clean if…

  • 1

    Windows look cloudy, spotted, or hazy even after rain — that is mineral deposit and oxidation buildup.

  • 2

    Hard-water streaks and calcium deposits building up over multiple seasons — these bond to glass and get harder to remove the longer they sit.

  • 3

    Spring pollen and fall insect debris visibly reducing natural light coming into the rooms.

  • 4

    Getting the home ready to sell — clean windows are one of the first things buyers notice in listing photos and in person.

FAQ

Window cleaning questions.

Do you clean interior windows too?

Our standard service covers exterior windows. Interior cleaning is available by request — let us know during the quote and we can include it.

Will low-pressure washing damage window seals or weatherstripping?

No. We use low pressure specifically to protect seals, frames, and caulking. Directing high-pressure water at window seals forces water into the frame cavity — that is not how we approach glass.

How often should windows be professionally cleaned in Champaign County?

Once or twice a year is typical for most homes. Spring clears winter grime and pollen; a fall clean removes summer dust and insect residue before the windows get sealed up for winter. Our blog post on how often to pressure wash covers this in more detail.

Do you do commercial window cleaning?

Yes — we handle storefront glass and commercial property windows. After-hours scheduling is available so we stay out of the way of your customers. See our commercial pressure washing service for full details.
