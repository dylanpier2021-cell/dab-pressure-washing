What's included

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Standard concrete cleaning covers the full surface area quoted. Rust pre-treatment and algae post-treatment are add-ons priced separately and called out in the quote.

Pre-treatment

Oil, rust, and organic stains get a targeted pre-treatment. Old, deeply soaked stains may lighten rather than fully disappear — we tell you up front.

Surface-cleaner pass

Flat surface cleaner distributes pressure evenly across the slab. No wand zebra lines. Consistent results across the whole surface.

Edge and joint detail

Expansion joints, step edges, and corners get hand-detailed with a wand where the surface cleaner can't reach.

Final rinse

Surface rinsed clean. Adjacent landscaping and grass rinsed of any debris pushed off the concrete.

Optional post-treatment

For surfaces with chronic algae from shade or moisture, a post-treatment application slows regrowth.

Walk-around

Before we leave, we walk the surface with you. Anything that didn't come up right gets another pass.

FAQ

Concrete cleaning questions.

What concrete surfaces do you clean?

Patios, sidewalks, porches, pool decks, and garage floors. For driveways specifically, see our driveway cleaning service — the technique is the same but driveways are quoted and scoped separately.

Will pressure washing etch or damage the concrete?

Not when done with a surface cleaner at the right pressure. We use a flat surface cleaner, not a wand, which distributes pressure evenly. Aggressive wand-only cleaning at close range can etch concrete — that is not our approach.

Can you remove algae from a patio that is slippery?

Yes. Algae on concrete patios is one of the most common jobs we do. It comes off fully in most cases, and we can apply a post-treatment to slow regrowth if the patio has chronic shade and moisture.

How long does it take to dry before I can use the patio?

A few hours in summer, longer if it is overcast. You can walk on it within an hour in most conditions.
