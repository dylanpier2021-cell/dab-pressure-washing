Commercial · Champaign, IL

Commercial pressure washing
for Champaign-County businesses.

Commercial pressure washing is a different job than residential — different access, different schedules, and a property manager who needs proof of insurance before anyone shows up. We handle storefronts, sidewalks, dumpster pads, drive-thrus, and parking-lot cleaning across Champaign County. We can schedule overnight or before opening, and we send a Certificate of Insurance to your property manager before the first visit.

What's included

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Commercial work is quoted custom. Most accounts run as a recurring monthly or quarterly schedule once the first clean sets the baseline.

Site walk

We walk the property with you or your property manager and put the scope in writing — what is being cleaned, how often, and at what price.

Storefront and sidewalk

Front entrance, sidewalks, and any high-traffic concrete around the building. Gum removal can be added in.

Dumpster pad

Dumpster pads get a degrease and surface-clean. This is usually the dirtiest part of the property and the easiest place to make a visible difference.

Drive-thru lane

Drive-thru lanes and order-window areas get cleaned with after-hours scheduling so we are not in the way of customers.

Recurring schedule

Monthly or quarterly schedules are common — once we know the property, the pricing settles in and you stop thinking about it.

Certificate of Insurance

Property managers get a COI emailed before the first visit, on request.

Signs it's time

You probably need commercial pressure washing if…

  • 1

    Black mildew streaks running down the exterior of the building.

  • 2

    Sidewalks at the front door look stained — gum, drink spills, food traffic.

  • 3

    Algae or mildew on the north-facing wall is starting to be visible from the parking lot.

  • 4

    A property manager or franchise inspector flagged exterior cleanliness in the last walk-through.
