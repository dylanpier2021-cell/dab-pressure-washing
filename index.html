FAQ

Questions, answered.

Don't see yours? Get in touch or call us anytime.

Does pressure washing damage siding?

Not when it is done correctly. Houses get a low-pressure soft wash with a cleaning solution that does the work, not the pressure. High-pressure washing on vinyl, wood, or painted siding can force water behind the surface or strip paint — that is not how we approach a house.

How long does a typical job take?

A standard single-story house wash runs about two to three hours. Driveways depend on size and stain levels, usually one to two hours. Decks and fences vary based on square footage and condition. We give a realistic time window during the quote.

Do I need to be home?

Not for most jobs, as long as we have access to outdoor water. Many homeowners prefer to be there for the walk-through at the end so we can show you the work and address anything before we leave.

What about my plants and landscaping?

We pre-soak nearby plants before applying any cleaning solution and rinse them again afterward. The mixes we use for soft washing are diluted to safe levels for landscaping when handled this way. We will mention anything that needs covered before we start.

Do you offer recurring service?

Yes — most commercial accounts run on a monthly or quarterly schedule, and we offer maintenance plans that save 10–20% off recurring service. Homeowners can book annual or seasonal cleans as well. Ask about it in your quote.
