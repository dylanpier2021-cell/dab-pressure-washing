What we noticed early on: most pressure washing in this area was being done as a side service by handyman crews and lawn companies. Sometimes that goes fine. A lot of the time it doesn't — too much pressure on soft siding, too little patience on stained concrete, no insurance when something goes wrong.

So we built DAB to do one thing well, with the right gear, the right pressure for each surface, and a price you actually agree to before we start.

## Section: What we believe

**Use the right pressure.** A house isn't a driveway. Vinyl siding isn't brick. We adjust pressure, tip, and chemistry for what we're actually cleaning — soft wash for siding, surface cleaner for concrete, controlled pressure for wood. That's why your house won't look streaked when we leave.

**Tell you the price first.** No "we'll figure it out when we get there." We walk the property, we tell you what it costs, we put it in writing. You decide.

**Show up on time.** If we say Tuesday at 9, we're there Tuesday at 9. If something changes, we call before we're late.

**Stay in our lane.** If a job needs roof replacement, deck staining, or anything outside what we do well, we'll tell you. We'd rather refer you out than do something half-right.
