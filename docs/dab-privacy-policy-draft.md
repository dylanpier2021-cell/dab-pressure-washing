## What information we collect

When you use our Site or contact us, we may collect:

**Information you give us directly.** When you submit a quote request form, message us through chat, or call/text us, you may give us your name, email, phone number, service address, and any details about the work you're asking us to do.

**Information collected automatically.** When you visit the Site, our hosting provider and analytics tools may automatically log standard technical information including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and approximate location (city/region). This is used to keep the Site working and improve it.

**Cookies.** The Site uses cookies and similar technologies for basic site functionality, analytics, and (where applicable) advertising performance measurement. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

We use the information you give us to:

## SMS and text messaging

If you give us your phone number through the Site, by phone, or in person, you agree we may contact you by call or text about your quote, scheduling, or service. Standard message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of marketing messages at any time by replying **STOP**. Replying STOP to a transactional/service message may also stop service-related texts — if it does, we may follow up by phone or email instead.

## Who we share information with

We share your information only with service providers who help us run the business, including:

- **GoHighLevel** — our customer relationship management (CRM), web form, chat, and SMS platform
- **Hosting and analytics providers** — to deliver the Site and understand basic usage
- **Payment processors** — when you pay an invoice, your card data is handled directly by the processor (we never see or store full card numbers)
- **Government or legal authorities** — if we are required to share information by law (subpoena, court order, etc.)

We do not share your information with anyone for their own marketing purposes.

## Your choices

You can:

- **Update or delete your info.** Email us and we will update or delete your contact information from our records, except where we are required to keep it (for example, for tax/invoicing records).
- **Opt out of marketing.** Reply STOP to any marketing text, click "unsubscribe" on any marketing email, or just tell us.
- **Decline cookies.** Use your browser's settings to block or delete cookies. The Site will still work for the most part.

If you are an Illinois resident, you have certain rights regarding your personal information. Contact us using the info at the top of this policy and we will help.
