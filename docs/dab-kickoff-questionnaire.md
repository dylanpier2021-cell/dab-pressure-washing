## Trust & proof (10 minutes — biggest impact on conversions)

18. Do you have a Google Business Profile? Share the URL.
19. How many Google reviews do you currently have, and what's the average?
20. Pick 5 happy past customers we can ask for permission to quote on the site. We need: their first name + last initial, the city they live in, and a one-sentence quote in their words. (We will not write fake reviews.)
21. Do you have general liability insurance? What's the coverage amount? (We can say "fully insured up to $X".)
22. Do you have a Certificate of Insurance you can send commercial leads on request?
23. Any certifications or training? (PWNA member, UAMCC member, manufacturer training, etc.) Don't list anything you don't actually have.
24. Average response time when a lead requests a quote? (If <2 hours, that's a quote-worthy stat.)
25. Roughly how many jobs have you completed lifetime? (Even a rough estimate is fine — "over 200" beats nothing.)
